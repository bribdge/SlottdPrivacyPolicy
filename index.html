Slottd - Privacy Policy

First Posted: 7/28/2026

Last Updated: 7/28/2026


This Privacy Policy explains Slottd's practices regarding user data.


1. Data Collection

Slottd does not collect, transmit, sell, or store any personal data or usage analytics. 100% of the user's data stays on their device, except as described in Section 5 (TestFlight Analytics) for users accessing the app via Apple TestFlight. If you wish to permanently clear all your on-device data, uninstall the app.


2. Processing of your Data

All functions of Slottd that use your user data, including AI features, occur entirely on-device and offline. On-device AI models, as used in Slottd, do not require contact with a server of any kind.


3. Children's Privacy

Slottd does not collect user data on anyone, regardless of age. Slottd and its content are not directed at children under the age of 13.


4. Changes to this Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time. The most current version of the policy will govern our use of your information and will always be at https://bribdge.github.io/SlottdPrivacyPolicy/. Because this policy may be frequently updated, we encourage you to check it often. By continuing to access or use Slottd after changes to this policy take place, you agree to be bound by the revised Privacy Policy.


5. GDPR-CCPA

Because Slottd does not collect, store, or transmit personal data, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, delete, or share upon request. If this changes in a future update, this policy will be revised accordingly to reflect applicable user rights under GDPR and CCPA.


6. TestFlight Analytics

This section applies only while the app is distributed through Apple TestFlight and does not apply to the public App Store release.

If you are accessing this application through Apple TestFlight, your crash logs and usage information will be automatically collected by Apple and shared with the Application Provider. Crash logs and usage information shared with Slottd is used solely to identify and fix bugs during the beta testing period, and is not linked to other data or shared with third-parties. More info on beta testing data is available at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/test-flight/.


7. Calendar, Camera, and Photo Library Access

While using Slottd, you may be asked for permission to access:

  • Your full Apple Calendar, including the ability to read, write, and edit events
  • Your camera, to take photos that remain inside the app
  • Pictures from your Photo Library

This data is used for basic functions such as adding/editing clothing items and generating outfits for the user. None of this data is stored, transmitted, or sold by Slottd, and all of it remains entirely on-device.


8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out to us at: appslottd@gmail.com
